========================================
 E X A M P L E S
========================================

 Pull one down and run it. On a board
 the browser saves it for you; from a
 desktop the link is a plain file.

 ---- hello.jdb -----------------------

 The smallest one there is, and the
 one that proves the download worked.

 [hello.jdb](progs/hello.jdb)

 ---- life.jdb ------------------------

 Conway on the console, forty columns
 wide, in about thirty lines. Arrays
 hold the board; the rules are the
 short part.

 [life.jdb](progs/life.jdb)

 ---- stars.jdb -----------------------

 A starfield that erases itself in
 black and draws itself again. What
 every board demo starts as.

 [stars.jdb](progs/stars.jdb)

 ---- sysinfo.jdb ---------------------

 What the machine is: chip, clock,
 memory, screen, network. Runs on
 every board and says what is missing
 rather than falling over.

 [sysinfo.jdb](progs/sysinfo.jdb)
