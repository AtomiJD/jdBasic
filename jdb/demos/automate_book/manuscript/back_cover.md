# Give your week back to yourself

Every office week hides a few hours of the same small chores: sorting
downloads, renaming scans, copying numbers from one sheet into another,
writing the same mail again, building the Friday report.

This book hands those chores to small programs that run on your own
computer. Fifteen easy recipes start with a setup wizard and no
programming at all. Fifteen medium recipes build invoices, letters,
calendars and reports. Fifteen expert recipes run on their own with logs,
retries, tests and an AI assistant.

Every program in the book was tested before it was printed, and every
recipe tells you how many minutes it gives back. What you do with them is
up to you.

The book is free to read as PDF and e-book. It was written in Markdown,
and jdBasic built every page of it.
