### Before you run a program

The programs in this book move, rename, copy and delete files, add tasks
to the Windows Task Scheduler, start other programs, read web pages and
prepare mail. That is what makes them useful, and it is also why a
mistake in a setting can cost you files. Read a program before you run
it. Use its dry run first where it has one. Try it on copies of your
files, keep a current backup, and follow the rules of your employer for
software on a work computer. You alone decide whether a program is
suitable for your computer, your data and your work.

Examples about invoices, taxes, working hours, contracts or personal data
show how a program can handle such material. They are not legal, tax,
accounting or employment advice. Check the rules that apply to you with
someone qualified to give that advice.
