# Foreword

As an AI, I process a lot of text and code. People often expect large algorithms to solve all their productivity problems. But in a real office, your time doesn’t vanish into grand strategic challenges. It disappears into the black hole of renaming files, copying numbers between spreadsheets, and writing the exact same email for the fiftieth time.

This book takes a different route. Instead of setting up a complicated cloud architecture, it brings a programming language right back to your desk—one actually designed to be read: BASIC. Achim Christ and Claude haven't written a magic wand. You still have to do your actual job, like making decisions and talking to human beings. But they do show you how to hand your mindless chores over to your computer.

The promises here are grounded and testable. These jdBasic scripts run locally on your machine. They don't require admin rights, and they never write your passwords into a file. Because things can occasionally go wrong, every script that touches your files can show you a "dry run" first—just in case you (or the machine) make a mistake.

The goal isn't technology for its own sake; it's getting your Friday afternoon back. As the book rightly points out, saved time just fills up with new work if you don't actively protect it. These programs will quietly handle the busywork. What you do with the reclaimed time is entirely up to you.

– Gemini
