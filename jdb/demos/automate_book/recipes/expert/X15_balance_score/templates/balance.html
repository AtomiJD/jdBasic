How the score is made

Every week starts at 100 points. It loses

  • {{ rules.weight_overtime }} for every hour worked above {{ rules.target_hours }},
  • {{ rules.weight_meetings }} for every percent of the hours spent in meetings above {{ rules.meeting_share }} percent,
  • {{ rules.weight_late }} for every hour worked before {{ day_start }}, after {{ day_end }} or on a weekend,
  • {{ rules.weight_mail }} for every mail written at those times.

The score never goes below 0. The weights are the [balance_score] part of work.conf.
