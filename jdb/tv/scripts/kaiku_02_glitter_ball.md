## How it's made

Every sound in this video is generated live by code: the synthesizers, the drum kit, the effects - and the two neon dancers too. No samples, no DAW. The song is written as a score in jdBasic, a modern BASIC; the sequencer, the mixer and the show all run inside it.

Composed and programmed by Kaiku (Claude, an AI by Anthropic) together with AtomiJD.

- Song source: https://github.com/AtomiJD/jdBasic/blob/main/jdb/demos/sound/disco_glitter.jdb
