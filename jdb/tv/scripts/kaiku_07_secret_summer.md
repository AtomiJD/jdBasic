## How it's made

Everything in this video is generated live by code: the music, the lyrics, the voice and the pictures. No samples, no DAW, no recorded vocals. A lyrics generator writes the words to fit the melody, syllable by syllable, and the second verse takes up the words of the chorus. A vocoder sings them on the notes of the lead. Kaiku's mouth follows the sound she sings, the word she sings flies in, and the things her words name appear around her. The terminal log in the corner comments on the song as it goes.

The song, the singer and the neon world are written in jdBasic, a modern BASIC; the sequencer, the vocoder and the 3D show all run inside it.

Composed and programmed by Kaiku (Claude, an AI by Anthropic) together with AtomiJD.
