# Magic in the Kingdom

Magic exists. It is rare, hard, and slow. Most people in the kingdom
have never seen a working spell. Those who have, usually only saw it
once.

The southern academy was the only place that taught the discipline
openly. It had perhaps thirty masters and seventy apprentices at its
height. It was closed by royal order two winters ago. The official
reason was "pending review". The apprentices were sent home. Two
masters were arrested. The rest are unaccounted for.

Maelyn was one of the apprentices. She was expelled six months before
the closure, for "asking the wrong questions about the source of
magic". She does not say what those questions were, even when asked
directly. She left with her notebooks and a small box of unsorted
samples.

# What Magic Costs

Magic draws from somewhere. The academy taught that the somewhere was
within the practitioner, drawn slowly from will and rest. Maelyn was
expelled, in part, for arguing that the academy was wrong about this -
that the somewhere was external, and that the practitioners had no
idea what they were drawing from.

This is the kind of question that gets one expelled.

# Moon-Herb

Grows only on south-facing slopes of these hills. The leaves close
shortly after sunset and reopen under moonlight. Picked between full
dark and second moonlight (about an hour), the leaves retain a faint
luminescence and a peculiar taste. Picked at any other time, they are
ordinary herbs.

The academy's standard texts described moon-herb as "useful in
preparations for calming and minor sight". Maelyn believes the texts
were wrong, or deliberately misleading. She has been collecting
samples to test on her own.

She needs three sprigs of correctly-picked moon-herb to complete the
test she has in mind. She has not been able to gather them herself
because the slopes at dusk are not safe alone since the wolves became
bolder.

# Things That Are Not Magic

People say a lot of things are magic. Most are not.

- The mist over the river at dawn: not magic.
- The way Hilda's forge fire stays lit through the night: a banked
  fire and a draft trick.
- The silence around the old keep: unusual, but not necessarily magic.

What might be magic, in Maelyn's careful opinion, is the keep itself,
and whatever made the tracks Tylen has been finding.
