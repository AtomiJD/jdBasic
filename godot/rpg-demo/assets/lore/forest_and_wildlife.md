# Quick Reference - The Forest of the Eastern Hills

- Wolves have grown bolder since King Aldred III's army withdrew two
  winters ago. They take livestock from the river village now.
- Strange tracks appear at the river bend - larger than wolf, no
  claws visible, always heading north-northeast toward the old keep.
- Tylen the ranger has found these tracks. He has not told Sir
  Gareth or Maelyn yet.
- Animals avoid the old keep. Deer trails detour around its base.
- The bandits who used to camp on the southern slopes are gone now.

# The Forest

The hills are mostly forested. Oak and pine on the slopes, birch in
the sunlit pockets, hawthorn at the forest edges. The undergrowth is
manageable - tracking is possible, with care.

# Wolves

Wolves have always lived in these hills. They were rare during the
war years - the army's foragers thinned them out. Since the
withdrawal, two winters ago, they have come back. They are bolder than
they were. They take livestock from the river village now and then.
This was not the case a generation ago.

Tylen has hunted them. Three pelts hang at his camp - the oldest from
the spring before last. They are large for the breed.

# Other Tracks

In the last six months, Tylen has found tracks at the river bend that
he cannot place. Larger than wolf. Longer stride. No claws visible -
the prints are smooth-padded, almost catlike but the wrong shape and
the wrong size. The tracks go north-northeast every time, never the
other way.

He has not told Sir Gareth. The knight would either dismiss him or
try to mount a patrol with men who don't read tracks. Neither outcome
helps. He has not told Maelyn either. She would ask to come along.

He told the player, if the player asks. Cautiously.

# Reading the Land

Tylen's rules, as he gives them to anyone who asks:

- The forest tells you what it tells you. It doesn't lie. It also
  doesn't volunteer.
- Tracks at the same place mean a pattern. Tracks at the same time
  mean a creature with a schedule. The river bend has both.
- Animals avoid the keep. So do birds, at certain times.
- The bandits used to camp on the southern slopes. They don't anymore.
  Not because they were driven out. Because they left.

# Hunting Quests Tylen Might Give

If the player has earned a measure of trust:

- Walk the northern ridge and report what is seen. No fighting. Just
  observation.
- If the player returns with anything more than birds and squirrels,
  Tylen will have more to say. If the player returns with nothing,
  he will nod and not press.

If the player asks for wolf pelts (for Sir Gareth), Tylen will direct
them to where the wolves den. He will not hunt them himself - he says
the wolves are not the problem.
