# Quick Reference - The Eastern Hills

Key places in the region of the Eastern Hills under King Aldred III:
- The crossroads: central meeting point of the old garrison paths.
- The river village: three days' walk west, on the river Marrow.
  Run by the blacksmith Hilda by default.
- The old keep: half a day's climb north up the peak. Abandoned,
  watched warily by everyone.
- The southern slopes: where moon-herb grows at dusk.
- The river bend: half a day east, where strange tracks appear.

# The Eastern Hills

The hills run roughly north-south for thirty leagues. Mixed forest -
mostly oak and pine, with stands of birch on the south-facing slopes.
The terrain rolls gently in the middle and steepens at both ends.

A central crossroads sits where the two old garrison paths meet. The
crossroads is where most travelers stop. There is no inn, only a
clearing and a memorial stone for those lost in the last campaign.

# The River Village

Three days' walk west of the crossroads. Sits on the southern bank of
the Marrow, the river that gives it its name. About forty households.
The blacksmith Hilda has the largest hearth. She trades for everything
that comes through.

The village has no garrison since the withdrawal. It has not been
raided. Travelers think this is luck. Hilda thinks it's because there
is nothing worth taking.

# The Old Keep on the Northern Peak

Visible from the crossroads on a clear day as a notched outline against
the sky. Half a day's climb. The path up has not been maintained.

The keep itself is roofless in three of its four wings. The fourth
appears intact at a distance. No smoke has ever been seen from it,
even on the coldest nights. Animals avoid it. Tylen has remarked that
the deer trails make a half-league detour around the base of the peak.

Officially the keep is "of historical interest, not currently
maintained". No one volunteers to maintain it.

# The Southern Slopes

The south-facing slopes catch the late sun and grow herbs that don't
appear elsewhere in the hills. Moon-herb grows there at dusk - the
leaves close after dark and reopen only in moonlight, which is where
the name comes from. Maelyn says the herb's properties have to do with
the slow heat of these slopes after sunset.

The slopes are also where most of the bandit camps used to be, before
the war. None now. The slopes are quiet.

# The River Bend and the Tracks

Half a day east of the crossroads, the Marrow bends sharply south. The
bend has a small pebble beach. Tylen has found tracks there - larger
than wolf, longer in the stride, no claw marks. He has not told
anyone other than the player who asks. The tracks reappear every few
weeks, always in the same direction (north-northeast, toward the
keep). He does not follow them.
