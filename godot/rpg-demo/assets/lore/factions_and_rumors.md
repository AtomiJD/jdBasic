# Quick Reference - People in the Eastern Hills

Under King Aldred III's distant rule, the Eastern Hills are governed
by no one in particular. The people who matter:
- Sir Gareth: a knight who stayed after the army withdrew.
- Maelyn: a young scholar of the arcane, former academy apprentice.
- Tylen: a ranger and hunter who reads the forest.
- Vex: a wandering trader with a shifty past, runs sealed parcels.
- Hilda: the blacksmith of the river village, de facto mayor.

# Who Is Who in the Hills

The hills have no formal authority since the withdrawal. The people
who matter are the people who know things or have things.

- **Sir Gareth** - the only knight who stayed. Patrols the eastern
  slopes. Trusts the king less than he used to. Drinks alone.
- **Maelyn** - former academy apprentice. Knows magic. Has a price
  on her head in the southern provinces, but not the local ones.
- **Tylen** - ranger from a village that no longer exists. Reads
  the forest. Doesn't trust easily.
- **Vex** - trader, sometime fence. Used to run with a band out of
  the river city. They scattered. He kept the contacts.
- **Hilda** - blacksmith of the river village. Knows everything that
  passes through her forge. Discreet.

# Wanted

Sir Gareth carries a list of names wanted by the crown. It is not
public. He keeps it folded inside his belt pouch. The names he is
most interested in:

- **"Half-Ear"** - a man Sir Gareth doesn't know by face. Wanted for
  smuggling weapons during the war. Vex has met him. Vex has not
  said so.
- **The Master Without a Name** - one of the arrested academy masters
  who slipped his guard. Description vague. Maelyn knows who it is.
  She has not said.

The crown does not pay for these names anymore. Sir Gareth still
keeps the list.

# What Vex Trades

Vex's stock is what fits in his pack and a strapped bundle. Common
items: candles, salt, small knives, lengths of rope. Less common: a
fold of letters, occasionally a sealed parcel he is carrying for
someone else for a fee. He never asks what is in a sealed parcel.

He has a current contract: a sealed parcel for Maelyn. The parcel
came from somewhere south. He doesn't say from whom. He is being
paid on delivery. He cannot find Maelyn himself because his patrol
of the hills doesn't bring him close enough to her camp, and because
he prefers to use a courier - safer that way.

# Rumors Worth Hearing

- The keep on the northern peak has had smoke from one of its
  chimneys, twice in the last month. People who saw it didn't agree
  on which chimney.
- A column from the river city tried to enter the hills last spring
  and turned back. No one wants to talk about why.
- Hilda the blacksmith has been working on something her usual
  customers can't afford. She doesn't say what.
- Maelyn's old academy is being "reorganized" by someone, somewhere.
  No public announcement.
