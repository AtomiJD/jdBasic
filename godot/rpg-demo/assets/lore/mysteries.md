# Open Threads

These are the things no single character in the hills knows fully.
Each character holds a piece.

# The Keep on the Peak

- It is not empty. Something is there.
- Smoke from one chimney, twice in the last month (Vex's rumor).
- Wildlife avoids it. Deer trails go around it (Tylen).
- A column from the kingdom went up three years ago and did not
  come back, except for survivors who would not speak of it
  (the chronicles, and Sir Gareth's silence).
- Maelyn suspects the keep is the source of whatever has been
  affecting the magical climate of the region. She hasn't said this
  out loud yet.

What this is, the demo does not commit to. Possibilities the NPCs
might allude to: a remnant of the war, a survival of the old academy
in hiding, a creature, a person. None of them know.

# The Tracks

Tylen has been finding them at the river bend. Larger than wolf,
longer stride, no claws visible, always heading north-northeast
toward the keep.

They are connected to whatever is in the keep. Tylen suspects but does
not say. He told the player if asked.

# The Sealed Parcel

Vex is carrying a sealed parcel for Maelyn. He doesn't know what is
in it. He has been paid for delivery only.

The parcel comes from someone south of the hills - a contact Vex
inherited from his old crew, someone who pays in good coin and asks
no questions in return. Maelyn does not know who is sending her this
parcel. The handwriting on the outer wrapping is unfamiliar. She has
suspicions about who it might be.

The contents (if the player ever finds out): a single sealed letter
and three vials of distilled herb essence. The letter is from a
former academy colleague who slipped capture two winters ago. He is
calling in old debts.

# The Wolves Becoming Bolder

Coincidence with the magical climate shift, or cause-and-effect?
Maelyn would argue cause. Tylen would argue the wolves are just
opportunistic since the army left. Sir Gareth would shrug.

# Hilda's Project

The blacksmith of the river village has been forging something for a
client who pays in silver instead of trade. She doesn't say what. The
client is someone she would not ordinarily work for.

This may or may not connect to the rest. The demo keeps this thread
open.
